Privacy policy

rclone-backup is used only by its owner, the GitHub user Wwaste, to back up their own files.

When the owner authorizes it, the app accesses only that same owner's Google Drive, and only to upload, list and verify the owner's own backup files. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data with anyone. Nothing is sent anywhere other than the owner's own Google Drive, and no analytics or tracking are used.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

隐私政策

rclone-backup 只由其所有者（GitHub 用户 Wwaste）本人使用，用来备份本人的文件。授权后，它只访问同一位所有者的 Google Drive，而且只用于上传、列出和核对本人的备份文件。它不收集、不存储、不出售，也不向任何人分享任何数据；除了所有者本人的 Google Drive，不向任何地方发送数据，也不做任何统计或追踪。所有者可以随时在上面的链接里撤销授权。
